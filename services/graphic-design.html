Why build with Lumencraft

Graphic Design has been core to what we do for 15 years. We've delivered hundreds of projects, many for businesses right here in South Florida, from Fort Lauderdale to Boca Raton to Miami, and we've formed strong opinions along the way. The biggest one: this work exists to move the business. Looking good is the price of entry; the real measure is how many people end up calling, booking and buying.

Everything under one roof

Good graphic design is really several projects travelling together. There's the strategy that decides who we're talking to and what they need to hear. There's the creative work where your brand comes to life. There's the build, the launch, and then the part most agencies skip: measuring what happened and making it better.

Plenty of agencies do one or two of those well and outsource the rest, which is how projects end up delayed, disjointed and over budget. We handle the whole stack in-house, with the same small team carrying your project from kickoff to launch and beyond. You can explore each stage in detail through the pages in this section, or just hand us the whole thing and let us run the process we've refined for over a decade.

The Lumencraft way of working

We deliver in stages, on purpose. You'll see the strategy before we design, the design before we build, and the build before we launch. At every checkpoint you give feedback and we act on it, which means there's no dramatic reveal at the end where you hope for the best.

When you call, you get the people doing the work, not an account layer reading from a status report. Clients have told us this is the reason they stay for years after launch, and it's why so much of our work comes from referrals.

If your current setup embarrasses you even slightly, or it simply isn't producing, you're leaving money with your competitors. Let's build the version your business actually deserves.
